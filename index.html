About This Project

This capstone was a structural departure from typical iConsultancy projects. Rather than assigning each section to a different client, all 22 teams across both sections of INST490 collaborated on a single large-scale project for one client: the Southern Poverty Law Center's Alabama Office, supporting their Data & Research, Democracy–Voting Rights Litigation, and Policy teams.

Each team tackled a distinct analytical dimension — from voter turnout and transportation barriers to ADA accessibility, broadband access, and civic education materials — producing a coordinated suite of research findings, datasets, visualizations, and design deliverables.

All analyses are descriptive and informational in nature and do not involve partisan conclusions. Deliverables were submitted directly to SPLC as completed work products.

Section 103 (Black Belt) studied Barbour, Bullock, Butler, Choctaw, Crenshaw, Dallas, Greene, Hale, Lowndes, Macon, Marengo, Montgomery, Perry, Pike, Russell, Sumter, and Wilcox counties across three presidential election cycles (2016, 2020, 2024).

Section 105 (Gulf Coast) studied Baldwin, Clarke, Conecuh, Covington, Escambia, Mobile, Monroe, Washington, and surrounding counties — including coastal and inland communities with distinct demographic and civic infrastructure profiles.

Teams shared a unified data infrastructure, methodology standards, and client relationship — gaining experience with the coordination demands of a real multi-team consulting engagement.

Select Project Findings
01
~23,000 fewer ballots cast, 2020–2024
Black participation declined in 21 of 24 study counties. The white-Black participation gap reached a 16-year statewide high.
02
93% vote on a single Election Day — no early voting in Alabama
Every transportation, accessibility, and distance barrier becomes acute. Five of six lowest-income counties have no fixed-route transit.
03
Gulf Coast: 106+ polling sites classified by accessibility tier
Drive-time and demographic analysis across Mobile and Baldwin counties identified communities with compounded access burdens across transit availability, road conditions, and ADA compliance.
Section 103

Alabama Black Belt Counties

17 counties · 11 teams
Section Report Section Slides
BB-DIG
Regional Voting Data Integration & Governance

Identified, collected, cleaned, and standardized public datasets on civic participation and voting access. Built shared data infrastructure, naming conventions, and geographic identifiers used by all Black Belt teams.

BB-VOTE
Voter Turnout & Participation Trends

Analyzed voter turnout across three presidential election cycles (2016, 2020, 2024) in 17 Black Belt counties. Examined participation patterns by geography and demographic indicators; documented a 5.5pp turnout decline in key districts.

BB-PAD
Polling Place Access & Distance Analysis

Conducted distance-to-polling-place analysis using ArcGIS spatial methods. Mapped access patterns, documented closures and consolidations, and identified communities with the greatest geographic access burdens.

BB-TRAC
Transportation Barriers to Civic Participation

Analyzed transportation infrastructure and vehicle access data across the Black Belt. Identified Dallas (15.1% car-free) and Bullock (zero fixed-route transit) as highest-priority targets for Election Day transportation intervention.

BB-AACS
ADA Accessibility & Compliance at Polling Locations

Audited all 49 active Montgomery County polling locations using the DOJ ADA checklist via Google Earth and Street View. Found only 27 of 49 with verifiable accessible entrances; 18 of 49 lacked passenger drop-off areas.

BB-BAND
Broadband & Digital Access for Civic Participation

Mapped broadband availability and reliability data across the region. Identified communities where limited digital access compounds barriers to civic information, online registration, and election resources.

BB-CPRI
Composite Civic Participation Risk Index

Developed and documented a composite index synthesizing indicators from all Black Belt teams — turnout, transportation, ADA, broadband, and demographics. Ranked counties by access risk with transparent weighting documentation.

BB-VIZ
Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

Designed data visualizations and dashboards for civic participation data. Established a visual design style guide for SPLC reuse — covering typography, color palettes, chart selection, and accessibility standards.

BB-CCP
Community Context & Personas

Conducted secondary research to build community personas grounded in regional data. Identified cross-cutting themes affecting participation and produced a narrative synthesis connecting personas to civic access patterns.

BB-PORT
Policy & Organizational Translation

Translated analytic findings from all Black Belt teams into clear summaries for internal SPLC stakeholders and community-facing audiences. Built a crosswalk mapping findings to organizational, policy, and legal considerations.

BB-CPAD
Civic Participation Process & Access Design

Designed clear, nonpartisan informational materials for community members navigating civic participation processes. Produced journey maps, flowcharts, and public-facing materials tailored to Black Belt regional context.

Section 105

Alabama Gulf Coast Counties

Gulf Coast region · 11 teams
Section Report Section Slides
GC-DIG
Regional Voting Data Integration & Governance

Built the shared data foundation for Gulf Coast analysis — collecting, cleaning, and standardizing public datasets on civic participation and voting access across Mobile, Baldwin, and surrounding counties.

GC-VOTE
Voter Turnout & Participation Trends

Analyzed turnout patterns across the Gulf Coast region for three presidential election cycles, examining participation by geography and demographic indicators — including Latinx community voter patterns across Mobile and coastal counties.

GC-PAD
Polling Place Access & Distance Analysis

Conducted tract-level drive-time and demographic analysis across Mobile and Baldwin counties. Produced an ArcGIS StoryMap illustrating access disparities and identifying communities with compounded distance burdens.

GC-TRAC
Transportation Barriers to Civic Participation

Mapped transportation barriers and no-vehicle rates across 106+ Gulf Coast polling locations. Classified sites by accessibility tier and identified geographic clusters where Election Day transit gaps are most acute.

GC-AACS
ADA Accessibility & Compliance at Polling Locations

Assessed ADA accessibility indicators at Gulf Coast polling locations, identifying patterns in physical access barriers. Produced categorized accessibility summaries and visual findings for SPLC planning use.

GC-BAND
Broadband & Digital Access for Civic Participation

Analyzed broadband availability and adoption across Gulf Coast counties. Mapped digital access patterns relevant to civic participation information access, including community anchor institutions as alternative access points.

GC-CPRI
Composite Civic Participation Risk Index

Synthesized indicators from Gulf Coast teams into a transparent composite risk index. Ranked communities by access risk with documented weighting decisions, data gap handling, and visualizations for county-level comparison.

GC-VIZ
Data Visualization & Dashboard Design

Designed dashboards and data visualizations for Gulf Coast civic participation findings. Produced visual design rationale and reusable style guidelines covering chart selection, accessibility, and SPLC branding alignment.

GC-CCP
Community Context & Personas

Developed community personas and thematic analyses grounded in Gulf Coast regional data. Connected persona narratives to identified civic participation access patterns across Mobile, Baldwin, and surrounding counties.

GC-PORT
Policy & Organizational Translation

Translated Gulf Coast analytic findings into public-facing and internal summaries for SPLC stakeholders. Produced a crosswalk mapping findings to organizational, policy, and legal considerations relevant to Gulf Coast communities.

GC-CPAD
Civic Participation Process & Access Design

Designed clear, nonpartisan informational materials for Gulf Coast community members. Produced civic process journey maps, flowcharts, and public-facing design materials reflecting regional context and community literacy needs.
